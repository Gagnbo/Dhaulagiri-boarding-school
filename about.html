OUR STORY

A Tradition of Quality Education

Dhaulagiri Boarding School, located in Hemja-25, Pokhara, Nepal, is dedicated to providing quality education and nurturing well-rounded individuals. We believe in building strong foundations for a brighter future through academic excellence, positive values, and an inspiring learning community.

Established with a vision to serve our community, our school pairs contemporary pedagogical approaches with traditional ethics, encouraging students to develop intellectual curiosity, empathy, and leadership capabilities.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide an accessible and high-standard education that develops knowledge, moral character, critical thinking, and leadership in every student.

👁️ Our Vision

To be a center of educational excellence, empowering youth to succeed globally while remaining grounded in their culture and contributing positively to society.
